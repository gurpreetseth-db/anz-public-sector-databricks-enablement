# Demo Talk Track

## Core narration pattern

For every step in the walkthrough, use the same cadence:
1. describe the operational problem
2. show the Databricks move
3. translate it into a public sector outcome

## Opening line

"Today I do not want to give you a product tour. I want to show you how a modern public sector data platform helps agencies move from fragmented signals to coordinated action."

## Transition phrases by act

### Mission setup to governed source access
"Before we talk about AI, we need to acknowledge reality: most agencies already have systems of record. The first question is not how to replace them. The first question is how to work with them safely and quickly."

### Source access to ingestion
"Existing systems only give us part of the picture. During a live event, new files and field reports start arriving continuously, and that is where the platform has to absorb change without drama."

### Ingestion to transformation and quality
"Getting data in is not the hard part. Getting it trusted is the hard part. If we are going to support operational decisions, we need to prove this data is fit for purpose."

### Transformation to modelling
"Once the data is reliable, we can stop describing yesterday and start helping teams prioritise what needs attention next."

### Modelling to ontology
"Predictions alone are not enough if different teams mean different things by severity, response time, or containment. Shared meaning is part of trusted decision-making."

### Ontology to Genie
"Now that the data is curated and the language is aligned, we can widen access. The next step is letting leaders ask direct questions without waiting on an analyst queue."

### Genie to application
"The real test is whether this work reaches the people making operational decisions. Insights matter most when they show up inside the workflow, not in a separate analytics silo."

## Messaging reminders

* keep returning to citizen outcomes, response quality, and operational trust
* describe AI as assisted decision support, not autonomous action
* emphasise incremental modernisation over rip-and-replace
* connect governance to enablement, not just control
* mention cross-agency consistency whenever ontology appears

## Closing line

"What I want you to remember is not a list of features. It is that one platform can take us from raw multi-agency signals, to trusted shared understanding, to intelligence, to action."
